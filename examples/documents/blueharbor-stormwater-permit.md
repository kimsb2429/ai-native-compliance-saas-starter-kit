# Industrial Stormwater General Permit, Coverage No. SW-2023-0882

Permittee: Blue Harbor Logistics LLC
Facility: Blue Harbor Container Yard, 88 Pier Road
Coverage effective: 2023-09-01    Expires: 2028-08-31

## Section 1. Stormwater pollution prevention plan (SWPPP)

1.1 The permittee shall maintain a written SWPPP on site and review it annually.

## Section 2. Inspections and sampling

2.1 The permittee shall conduct a visual inspection of all outfalls once per quarter during a qualifying rain event and record the findings within 24 hours.
2.2 The permittee shall collect stormwater samples at Outfall 001 twice per year, once in the wet season and once in the dry season, and analyze for total suspended solids, oil and grease, and pH.

## Section 3. Reporting

3.1 The permittee shall submit an annual report to the regional water board by October 15 covering the prior monitoring year.
3.2 The Yard Operations Manager is the responsible party for inspections and reports.

## Section 4. Training

4.1 All yard staff shall complete stormwater pollution prevention training annually.
