# Air Quality Operating Permit AQ-2024-0117

Permittee: Acme Fabrication, Inc.
Facility: Acme Fabrication North Plant, 1400 Industrial Parkway
Issued: 2024-03-15    Expires: 2029-03-14
Issuing authority: State Department of Environmental Quality, Air Division

## Section 1. Emission limits

1.1 Particulate matter emissions from Stack S-1 (paint booth exhaust) shall not exceed 0.02 grains per dry standard cubic foot.
1.2 Volatile organic compound (VOC) emissions from the coating line shall not exceed 24.5 tons in any rolling 12-month period.

## Section 2. Monitoring and testing

2.1 The permittee shall conduct a performance (stack) test on Stack S-1 once every calendar quarter, using EPA Method 5, and submit results within 30 days of the test.
2.2 The permittee shall inspect the wet scrubber on the coating line at least once every six months and record the pressure drop and liquid flow rate.
2.3 The permittee shall calibrate the continuous opacity monitor annually, no later than the anniversary of the permit issue date.

## Section 3. Recordkeeping

3.1 The permittee shall keep a monthly log of coating usage, VOC content of each coating, and calculated VOC emissions. The log shall be completed by the 10th day of the following month.
3.2 All records required by this permit shall be retained on site for five years and made available to the Department on request.

## Section 4. Reporting

4.1 The permittee shall submit an annual emissions inventory report to the Department by March 1 of each year, covering the previous calendar year.
4.2 The permittee shall submit a semi-annual monitoring report by July 31 and January 31, summarizing all deviations from permit conditions.

## Section 5. Malfunction and deviation

5.1 The permittee shall notify the Department by telephone within 24 hours of any malfunction that causes emissions to exceed a limit in Section 1, and in writing within 7 days.
5.2 The Environmental Manager is the responsible party for all notifications under this section.

## Section 6. General conditions

6.1 The permittee shall apply for renewal at least 180 days before the expiration date.
6.2 Any change in process equipment that could increase emissions requires prior written approval.
